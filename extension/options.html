How to use

  • Open the gallery: tap the floating button on X, or the extension’s button in the toolbar.
  • Load everything: open x.com/i/bookmarks once; all your bookmarks load in the background. You can pause and continue.
  • Look at a post: tap a picture. Pinch (or tap the magnifier) to zoom; tap the arrow to see the post on X, then Back to return.
  • Save to a collection: tap the bookmark button. Long-press it, or double-tap the picture, to save to the collection you used last. Swipe a post left for the list.
  • Pick many: long-press a picture in the grid (or tap Select), tap more, then “Add to collection”.
  • Hide a post: the eye button hides it from All posts; it stays bookmarked and in its collections.
  • Find things: search words or @name, filter Photos / Videos / Unsorted, or browse by Months and Years.
  • Collections: search them, sort them, “Change order…” to drag them, and “Choose cover” in a collection’s ⋯ menu.
  • Videos: hold a video for 2× speed; the speed and sound buttons remember your choice.
  • Something wrong? In the gallery: ⋯ → “Check the connection to X”.
